# Tamil Nadu 2026 Assembly Election Analysis
## CODEBASICS — Resume Project Challenge

## Project Overview
A data-driven analysis of the 2026 Tamil Nadu Assembly Election results 
across all 234 constituencies. Built as a show pitch for AtliQ Media — 
a fictional news network producing a one-hour fact-based election show.

**Non-partisan disclaimer:** This project does not support, endorse, or 
criticise any political party. All findings are based purely on publicly 
available Election Commission of India data.

## Three Stories
- **The Great Flip** — 163 of 234 seats changed their winning party
- **The Geographic Sweep** — Every region of Tamil Nadu shifted
- **The Fragmented Mandate** — Most winners got less than half the votes

## Key Findings
- TVK won 108 seats in their very first election (party founded 2024)
- DMK retained only 40 of 133 seats — 30% survival rate
- BJP retained 0 of 4 seats
- Tiruppattur won by exactly 1 vote out of 200,000+ cast
- TVK won 52% of SC reserved seats vs 45% of general seats
